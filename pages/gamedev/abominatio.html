What I Think of ABOMINATIO

ABOMINATIO, as i said, is a VERY special game to me, yes, it fucking sucks as a game. But it was MY first game to ever see the light of day (well, if you don't count the Roblox ones.) so when I think of ABOMINATIO, I see it as my brave experiment, just saying "Fuck It" and making a game. The reason why ABOMINATIO was even made, is because of two factors, one is that I was invited to work on a game project with some friends, that game didn't go too far, but it put the logs on my heart to finally try make a game for real, a game that people could actually play and enjoy.. The other factor, was that PSYCHOPOMP GOLD was released at that time, I've loved the original PSYCHOPOMP, but PSYCHOPOMP GOLD, truly became one of, if not, my favorite Indie Game, and it is truly an inspiration, to me, to it's creator Karbonic, to it's many Surrealistic Inspirations (Surrealism being like... My autism) and many other things. PSYCHOPOMP, was made with the Godot Engine, and so was the project that i was working on, because of this, I decided that Godot, should be the engine to use, and I dont regret using it one bit.

The project was abandoned after the first chapter, I felt that it was too much with the experience I had at that moment, so after finishing the "first chapter" I put on Hiatus, that means that it's not truly abandoned, I absolutly plan on coming back and finishing it, but I have other projects now. But one day, we'll see ABOMINATIO finished.
